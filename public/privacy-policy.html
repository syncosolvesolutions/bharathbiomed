Privacy Policy — Bharath Biomed Pharma App

Last updated: 2026-07-25

This app is provided by Bharath Biomed Pharma ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Bharath Biomed Pharma mobile app collects and how it's used.

Data we collect

Account sign-in. If you sign in, we collect the email address you enter, via Firebase Authentication, solely to authenticate you and allow syncing the product catalog to your device. We do not collect your password (it's handled directly by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us).

Crash and diagnostic data. We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and basic device information (device model, OS version, and a device identifier) when the app crashes or encounters an error, so we can find and fix bugs.

Usage analytics. We use Firebase Analytics to collect basic, aggregated usage data (e.g. which screens are viewed) to understand how the app is used.

Product catalog data. The app downloads a product catalog (names, descriptions, images) from our Firebase database and stores it locally on your device so it can be viewed offline. This data is about our products, not about you.

How we use this data

  • To let you sign in and download the current product catalog.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To understand overall app usage and improve the app.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties other than our service providers (Google Firebase, which processes data strictly on our behalf to provide the services above).

Data retention and deletion

Catalog data cached on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of your account/sign-in data, contact us at swapna.balugu@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for business use by sales personnel and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
